C-UAS Swarm Behavioral Intent Classification Middleware

Most C-UAS platforms share the same structural gap: they trigger on physical perimeter breach. By that point, the response window for hard-kill or soft-kill countermeasures is already dangerously narrow.

The behavioral intent classification middleware I am presenting you is designed to sit downstream of your existing sensor stack - radar, RF/DF, optical, acoustic - and classify swarm intent before any physical threshold is crossed.

The system operates on a dual-layer architecture: an Operator HUD designed for immediate tactical decisions and a Forensic Analyst Console running in parallel that guarantees full transparency and regulatory auditability, without adding cognitive load to the operator during engagement.

CORE CAPABILITIES

  • Predictive Behavioral Alerting: the engine identifies hostile intent from formation geometry, convergence dynamics and heading evolution, delivering actionable alerts significantly earlier than proximity-based triggers across multiple threat profiles
  • Multi-scenario Threat Classification: validated across coordinated strikes, deceptive probing, encirclement patterns, decoy maneuvers, swarm splits, etc., each classified independently in real time
  • EW-Resilient False Positive Suppression: The classification layer is built to filter out active jamming clutter, hostile RF deception, atmospheric noise and sensor degradation before they reach the operator display, preserving decision authority under electronic attack
  • EW & Environmental Degradation Margins: Automatically scales tactical threat and risk buffers when sensors suffer high noise or degradation due to severe weather (dense fog, rain) or active electronic countermeasures, covering critical sensor-blindness gaps.
  • Deception & Spoofing Isolation: Dual-track classification continuously identifies kinematic spoofing (such as GPS manipulation, artificial speed deltas and coordinated decoy splits) from real targets using unsupervised Isolation Forest anomaly vectoring
  • Tactical-Contextual Risk Divergence & Live Diagnostics: Computes a dynamic operational risk score separating raw physical threat vectors from tactical reality, factoring in real-time sensor uncertainty, proximity-escalation physics and Rules of Engagement constraints (such as commercial flight path proximity). The engine instantly delivers human-readable diagnostics on the operator HUD explaining the exact cause of any risk mismatch (e.g., sensor degradation, dense fog or ROE adjustments) to support rapid, high-confidence decision-making
  • Class-Balanced Platt Scaling Calibration: mitigates positive-class imbalances using class-balanced scaling weights, drastically reducing false-alarm fatigue while resolving high-density coordinated threats cleanly above the operational action threshold
  • Calibrated Probabilistic Output: intent probabilities are calibrated for operational engagement decisions, not raw ML scores passed directly to the operator
  • Zero-Temporal-Leakage HMI: ensures telemetry compliance by maintaining active flying tracks in a strict PENDING state until terminal intersection, committing final outcomes (DEFEATED, LEAKED, PARTIALLY_INTERCEPTED) only upon physical track termination (LOST, IMPACT, TERMINATED) to eliminate operational hindsight bias
  • Automated Mitigation Recommendations: incorporates a cooperative tactical decision loop that suggests targeted countermeasures (such as automated High-Power Microwave beam activation and jammer handoffs) based on real-time swarm convergence velocities
  • EU AI Act Article 9 compliant by design: every engagement decision logged with a human-readable Audit Justification String to an immutable audit trail, fully auditable for command accountability
  • Air-Gap Deployable: built for on-premise or closed command center environments
  • SDK-Ready & Sensor-Agnostic Architecture: designed as an API-first, air-gap deployable middleware.
